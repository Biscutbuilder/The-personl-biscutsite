More about my plane project!

My Fixed winged Aircraft project is a hardware and CAD project that i have been working on for around 2 years now

The Aircraft will use 2 ESP32 microcontrollers that are using ESPNOW to communicate with eachother. One ESP will be a ground system and will take human inputs via joysticks and potentiomiter to control flight and thrust, ESPNOW will relay information to the Aircraft, that will have 4 Servos and BLDC motor controlled by the ESP32. The ESP inside the plane will exicute the commands from the other ESP.

The contraisnt on this project are the following: Final cost of under 150 USD, Use only ESPNOW to communicate, be able to contain a Arduino Uno to preform data collection, and the aircframe must be easy to decontruct and change parts.
